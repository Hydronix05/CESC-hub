About CESC Hub

rules, privacy, guides — everything you need to know

Community Rules
last updated: October 2026
1
Respect
treat every member with basic respect. no bullying, harassment, hate speech, or targeted mockery. disagreements are fine — attacks are not.
2
Content
no NSFW, gore, spam, or illegal content. this is a school community — keep it appropriate for all CESC students.
3
Accounts
one account per person. no impersonation of other students, teachers, or admins. keep your login private.
4
Posts & Chats
you own your words. post in the right place. don't share links to scams or malware. don't screenshot DMs without permission.
5
Moderation
breaking a rule gets you a warning. repeat offenses get you timed out. serious breaks — hate speech, doxxing, threats — lead to permanent bans.
6
Report Problems
if something feels wrong, report it. admins would rather hear about small issues than find out about big ones too late.

Privacy Policy
last updated: October 2026
What We Collect
your username, email, display name, bio, profile picture, class, section, roll number, gender, posts, comments, messages, and game activity on CESC Hub.
Why We Collect It
to run CESC Hub. that's it. no advertising beyond CESC Hub's own ad system. no selling data to third parties. no tracking across other websites.
Who Can See Your Data
your profile is visible to other CESC Hub members. your DMs are between you and the recipient. admins can only see your private data if a report requires investigation.
How It's Stored
on Supabase — secured with row-level security and encryption. passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text.
Cookies & Local Storage
we use localStorage for your theme, onboarding state, and short-lived post cooldown. no third-party cookies or tracking scripts.
Your Rights
you can edit or delete your profile at any time in Settings. to delete your account entirely, message an admin.

Terms of Service
last updated: October 2026
1
Who Can Use CESC Hub
current CESC students, teachers, and approved advertisers. accounts are created by CESC Hub admins. shared accounts are not allowed.

you own what you post. by posting on CESC Hub, you grant CESC Hub permission to display your content within the app. you can delete your own content at any time.

Admin Rights

Service Availability

Changes to Terms

Getting Started
your first 5 minutes on CESC Hub
1
Set up your profile
go to Settings → add a profile picture, write a short bio, choose a display name. this is how other CESC students will recognise you.
2
Say hi in the feed
open the Feed and tap Create Post. write something short — a hello, a thought, a meme. it'll show up for everyone.
3
Join the chat
click Chat. you'll land in the Global Chat — everyone in CESC. type a message and press Enter. that's it.
4
Try a game
visit Games. play Bloxd.io on the CESC Hub server — playtime counts toward the Leaderboard.
5
Install the app
add CESC Hub to your home screen. open the Downloads page for step-by-step instructions for your device.

The Feed
where everyone posts
1
Make a post
tap Create Post (top right of the feed). write what's on your mind, add an image if you want. no character limit.
2
Format your text
use the toolbar to make text bold, italic, underlined, or pick a size (small / normal / large / huge). you can also add links with the 🔗 button.
3
Like and comment
tap the ❤️ to like a post. tap 💬 to open comments. you'll get a notification when someone likes or comments on your posts.
4
Delete your own posts
on your own posts, tap the ⋯ menu → Delete. this removes the post and all its comments.

Chat
global chat, DMs, and how to reach people
1
Send a message in Global Chat
open Chat. Global Chat is open by default — everyone sees your messages. type and press Enter.
2
Start a direct message (DM)
use the search bar at the top of the chat inbox. type a name, tap the person, and start typing. your conversation is private — only you and they can see it.
3
Reply to a message
hover a message (or long-press on mobile) and tap ↩. you'll see a preview of what you're replying to.
4
Edit or delete your message
hover your own message and tap ✏️ to edit (within 15 minutes), or 🗑️ to delete. deleting hides it from everyone except the owner.
5
Post a note
notes appear at the top of the chat inbox. tap your own avatar to post a 24-hour note, like an Instagram story.

Profiles
browse and find CESC members
1
Browse members
open Profiles. every member appears as a card — name, avatar, roles, and last-seen. order is shuffled so everyone gets seen.
2
Switch views
use the toggle top-right to switch between cards and rows. whichever feels better to you.
3
Search for someone
type a username or display name in the search bar. matches appear instantly.
4
View a profile
tap any card to see their bio, role badges, join date, and last active time.

Games
play, compete, climb the leaderboard
1
Open the Games hub
click Games in the sidebar. pick a game — currently just Bloxd.io.
2
Play Bloxd.io
tap Play. on mobile you'll go full-screen. on desktop the game loads in an iframe below the toolbar. play on the CESC Hub server for it to count.
3
How playtime works
your session is tracked only while the game tab is active and you're clicking/moving. switch tabs or walk away and the timer pauses — that's fair for everyone.
4
Check your rank
visit the Leaderboard. playtime feeds into the Overall score alongside your login streak.

Leaderboard
4 ways to see who's on top
1
Overall score
combines your login streak (×15 per day) and your gameplay time (×0.5 per minute). streak is weighted more — consistency beats grinding.
2
Bloxd.io tab
pure playtime ranking. whoever played the most wins. no streak involved.
3
Streak tab
login streak leaderboard. log in every day and you climb. miss a day and you start over.
4
Last Online tab
not a competition — just who's been active most recently. good for seeing who's around.

Settings
make CESC Hub feel like yours
1
Choose a theme
7 theme families, each with a dark and light variant. pick your vibe. the moon/sun button in the header toggles dark ↔ light for whichever family you picked.
2
Set your chat layout
two layouts: Meta (WhatsApp-style, your messages on the right) or Discord (all messages left-aligned). pick whichever feels natural.
3
Change your profile
update your display name, bio, or profile picture. display name changes are limited to 2 per week — pick carefully.
4
Change your password
under Security, enter a new password twice. keep it at least 6 characters — longer is better.

Install the App
CESC Hub on your home screen, no app store needed
CESC Hub is a Progressive Web App (PWA) — that means you can install it to your home screen like a native app, without any app store. it works on every modern phone, tablet, and computer. chrome is recommended for the smoothest install and experience.
Android
Chrome · Edge · Samsung Internet
  1. open cesc-hub.vercel.app in Chrome
  2. tap the ⋮ menu (three dots, top right)
  3. tap "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen"
  4. confirm — the CESC Hub icon appears on your home screen
iPhone / iPad
Safari · Chrome (Safari recommended)
  1. open cesc-hub.vercel.app in Safari (or Chrome)
  2. tap the Share button (square with arrow, bottom of screen)
  3. scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. tap Add — the icon appears on your home screen
Windows
Chrome · Edge
  1. open cesc-hub.vercel.app in Chrome
  2. look for the install icon (small monitor with arrow) in the address bar
  3. click Install
  4. CESC Hub opens as its own window and appears in your start menu
macOS
Chrome · Safari
  1. open cesc-hub.vercel.app in Chrome or Safari
  2. in Chrome: look for the install icon in the address bar
  3. in Safari: File → Add to Dock
  4. CESC Hub opens as its own app
