About CESC Hub

Built by students, for students.

CESC Hub is a private community platform made specifically for students of Cantonment English School & College (CESC). It's a space where classmates can stay connected, share what they're thinking, discover what others are working on, and just be part of a community that knows them.

We built it because the tools most people use are too big, too noisy, and not really meant for a school. CESC Hub is smaller, calmer, and shaped entirely around the people it serves.

The School Behind the Hub

Cantonment English School & College

CESC stands for Cantonment English School & College — an English-version national curriculum institution in Chattogram Cantonment, Bangladesh.

The school started its journey in 1998 as Cantonment International School, became Cantonment English School in 2005, and was upgraded to college level in 2010, becoming CESC. It follows the National Curriculum in English version and is affiliated with the Chittagong Education Board.

CESC Hub exists to serve the students of this school — it's not the school's official platform, but a community built by students who wanted a space of their own.

Features

Everything the community needs.

A focused set of tools designed for real student life — nothing more, nothing less.

Community Posts

Share ideas, updates, and creative work with the community. Comment, react, and stay in the loop with what's happening.

Private & Group Messaging

Chat with classmates one-on-one, or join group conversations. Keep up with the community without the noise.

Student Profiles

Showcase who you are, what you're interested in, and what you're working on. A profile that's actually yours.

Explore & Discover

Find new posts, people, and ideas from across the community. Discover what others are creating and thinking about.

Notes & Notifications

Post quick 24-hour notes, and get notified when people interact with your content — the small moments that keep the community alive.

Bloxd Gaming

Jump into shared gaming sessions with classmates. Play together, track your playtime, and climb the community leaderboard.

CEIN Rewards

Earn CEIN — the community's reward currency — for being active, staying consistent, and contributing. A simple way to recognize participation.
